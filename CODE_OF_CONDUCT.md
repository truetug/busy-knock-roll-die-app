# Code of Conduct

Be kind and assume good faith. This project welcomes everyone who wants to improve it, whatever their background or experience.

Expected: respectful, patient and constructive communication; giving and accepting feedback gracefully; focusing on what is best for
the project and the people using it.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind; personal or political attacks; publishing other people's private
information; anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who do so
repeatedly. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile; reports are kept
confidential.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
